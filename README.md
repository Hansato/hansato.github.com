## 🇪🇺 EU Policy & Compliance

Understanding the regulatory landscape is the first step for any supply chain operating in or entering Europe.

- **Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA)** – Sets 2030 targets: 10% extraction, 40% processing, 25% recycling of 17 strategic raw materials. Diversification target: no single country >65% of EU demand. First 47 Strategic Projects designated in 2025.
- **RESourceEU Action Plan** – Adopted December 2025. Focuses on critical raw materials for clean energy, EVs, digital transition, and defence industries. Coordinates supply diversification and boosts domestic production.
- **Circular Economy Act (2026)** – Forthcoming legislation (Q3 2026) to foster secondary raw material recovery and strengthen Europe's strategic autonomy.
- **EU White Paper for European Defence – Readiness 2030** – Highlights CRMs as vital for economic production, defence capabilities, and competitiveness. EDA to identify most vital CRMs and suggest diversification.
- **NATO High Visibility Project (2025)** – 12 allies agreed on joint acquisition, storage, transportation, and stockpile management of defence-critical raw materials, including recycling.
- **SAFE Regulation (Security Action for Europe)** – Allows reservation of manufacturing slots and stockpiling of defence products, intermediate products, or raw materials for funding applications.
- **GeoERA & Geological Service for EU** – EU projects providing mineral data through EuroGeoSurveys' European Geological Data Infrastructure.
- **CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)** – The EU's tool to put a fair price on the carbon emitted during the production of carbon-intensive goods entering the EU.
- **EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation)** – Requires strict due diligence and geolocation data for commodities like wood, rubber, coffee, and cocoa to ensure they are deforestation-free.
- **CSDDD (Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive)** – Mandates large companies to identify, prevent, and mitigate human rights and environmental impacts in their global supply chains.
- **EU Battery Regulation** – Introduces the "Battery Passport," requiring detailed data on carbon footprint, recycled content, and supply chain due diligence.

## 📚 Frameworks & Models

- **SCOR Model (Supply Chain Operations Reference)** – Widely recognized framework for supply chain management, developed by ASCM. Covers plan, source, make, deliver, return, and enable processes.
- **Gartner Supply Chain Maturity Model** – Framework for assessing and advancing supply chain capabilities across process, organisation, technology, and performance dimensions.
- **DCOR Model (Design Chain Operations Reference)** – Framework for product development and design chain management, complementing SCOR.
- **CCOR Model (Customer Chain Operations Reference)** – Framework for customer engagement and order management processes.
- **Sustainable Supply Chain Framework (UNEP)** – UN Environment Programme guidelines for integrating sustainability into supply chain operations.
- **Blockchain for Supply Chain (EU Blockchain Observatory)** – Framework and guidelines for implementing blockchain solutions in logistics and traceability.
