## 🇪🇺 EU Policy & Compliance

Understanding the regulatory landscape is the first step for any supply chain operating in or entering Europe.

- **Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA)** – Sets 2030 targets: 10% extraction, 40% processing, 25% recycling of 17 strategic raw materials. Diversification target: no single country >65% of EU demand.
- **CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)** – The EU’s tool to put a fair price on carbon emitted during production of goods entering the EU. Transition period ended; full implementation active in 2026.
- **EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation)** – Requires strict due diligence and geolocation data for commodities like wood, rubber, coffee, cocoa, cattle, soy, and palm oil. *Note: Interpretive scope for bamboo/grasses is evolving.*
- **CSDDD (Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive)** – Mandates large companies to identify, prevent, and mitigate human rights and environmental impacts in their global supply chains.
- **GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation)** – Replaces the old MDD/LVD for non-harmonized products. Introduces strict requirements for Responsible Persons (RP), technical files, and batch traceability.
- **PPWR (Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation)** – Enters into force August 2026. Harmonizes packaging rules across the EU, banning certain single-use plastics and mandating recyclability design.
- **EU Battery Regulation** – Introduces the “Battery Passport,” requiring detailed data on carbon footprint, recycled content, and supply chain due diligence.
- **Digital Product Passport (DPP)** – Upcoming requirement for construction and textile products to provide machine-readable lifecycle data via QR/NFC carriers.

## 🌿 Sustainable Materials & Green Building

For architects, specifiers, and procurement teams targeting LEED, BREEAM, DGNB, or WELL certifications.

- **Engineered Bamboo Sourcing Guide** – Forensic verification protocols for moisture stability, adhesive REACH compliance, structural batch testing, EUDR traceability, EPD certification, and BIM integration. [Read Briefing](https://www.hansato.com/sourcing-engineered-bamboo-china-quality-compliance)
- **EPD Standards (ISO 14025)** – Type III Environmental Product Declarations required for green building credits. Verify suppliers hold valid EPDs published on recognized program operators (Environdec, IBU).
- **BIM Integration Requirements** – Open-format BIM objects (IFC 4.x, COBie) with embedded material properties and EPD linkage. Essential for public procurement and digital twin workflows.
- **Sustainable Construction Materials Hub** – Browse all Hansato briefings on eco-friendly sourcing and compliance. [View Topic](https://www.hansato.com/topics/strategic-sourcing)

## 📚 Frameworks & Models

- **SCOR Model (Supply Chain Operations Reference)** – Widely recognized framework for supply chain management, developed by ASCM. Covers plan, source, make, deliver, return, and enable processes.
- **Gartner Supply Chain Maturity Model** – Framework for assessing and advancing supply chain capabilities across process, organisation, technology, and performance dimensions.
- **Sustainable Supply Chain Framework (UNEP)** – UN Environment Programme guidelines for integrating sustainability into supply chain operations.
- **Blockchain for Supply Chain (EU Blockchain Observatory)** – Framework and guidelines for implementing blockchain solutions in logistics and traceability.
